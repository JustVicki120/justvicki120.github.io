Welcome!

Hi! I'm Vicki! 💙

I'm a wife, mom, grandmother, animal lover, and lifelong learner. I'm a former labor and delivery nurse who traded hospital hallways for local highways, and these days you'll often find me behind the wheel, meeting interesting people and collecting stories along the way.

I've always been curious about the world around me, and lately that curiosity has turned into something new: I build apps. What started as "I wonder if I could…" became Just Vicki, LLC, my little company that makes friendly, thoughtful apps for iPhone, iPad and, soon, Android.

Welcome to my little corner of the internet! 💙
