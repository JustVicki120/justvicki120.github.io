A Little About Me

Hi! I'm Vicki! 💙

I'm a wife, mom, grandmother, animal lover, and lifelong learner. I'm a former labor and delivery nurse who traded hospital hallways for local highways, and these days, you'll often find me behind the wheel, meeting interesting people and collecting stories along the way.

I've always been curious about the world around me. I love technology, creating and editing videos, dreaming up new projects, and figuring out how to make those ideas come to life. My latest adventure? Learning to build my own app!

I'm happiest when I'm learning something new, making something beautiful, spending time with the people I love, or enjoying the company of any horse or my beloved dogs and parrots.

I believe life is full of interesting people, unexpected opportunities, and wonderful connections just waiting to happen.

And that's why I'm here. Welcome to my little corner of the internet! 💙
