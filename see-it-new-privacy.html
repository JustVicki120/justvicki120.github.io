This policy explains how the ¡see it new! and KIDS ¡see it new! apps (together, "the apps") handle your information. The apps are made by Just Vicki, LLC, a Colorado limited liability company ("we," "us").

The short version
  • We do not collect, store, or see any of your personal information.

What Stays on Your Device

Everything you create in the apps is saved only on your iPhone, iPad, or Android device, including:

  • photos you take or choose for a quest,
  • the stories and memories you write or speak,
  • in KIDS ¡see it new!, voice recordings, only if a parent turns recording on,
  • your quest progress and settings, and
  • in KIDS ¡see it new!, the parent passcode and the grown-up question and answer.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it for you.

Permissions the Apps Ask For

  • Camera: to take photos for your quests.
  • Photos: to choose existing photos and, when you ask, to save photos to your library.
  • Microphone and speech recognition (KIDS ¡see it new!): so a child can speak a story instead of typing it. The words are turned into text on the device. If a parent has turned on voice recording, the recording is saved in the app on the device.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device's Settings. The apps still work without them; only the related feature is unavailable.

Backups and Sharing

  • Device backups: Your device may include the apps' data in its own backups (for example, iCloud Backup on Apple devices). Those backups are handled by your device's maker under your settings, not by us.
  • Export My Collection: When you export your collection, the file goes only where you choose to save it.
  • Sharing: Photos and stories leave the app only when you choose to share or save them. In KIDS ¡see it new!, sharing, saving outside the app, deleting, and changing settings require the parent passcode.

Children's Privacy

KIDS ¡see it new! is designed for children ages 7 to 12, and ¡see it new! may be used by people of any age. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or from anyone else. The apps have no accounts, no ads, no chat, and no way to contact other people, and grown-up features are protected by a parent passcode.

Deleting Your Information

You can delete individual photos and stories inside the apps. Deleting an app from your device removes everything stored in it, unless you have exported a copy or it remains in a device backup.

If You Email Us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you, and we do not share it.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how the apps handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
